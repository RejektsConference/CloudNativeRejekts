# The Organizing Team Must Contain Local Volunteers

**Status:** Approved

## Context

Cloud Native Rejekts is a community run event, by the community and for the community. As such we don't have the budget to cover travel expenses to allow organisers to seek out and evaluate suitable event venues, local suppliers or understand the local transport and accommodation options. We want to make sure we maintain the high quality that our attendees expect and continue to host Rejekts in locations that align with our values.

## Decision

For Cloud Native Rejekts to be go ahead in a location, the Organizing Team must contain volunteers from the local area in which the event is to take place - at least one volunteer but ideally more.

## Consequences

* **Pros:**
  * We can ensure that the venue is suitable for the event and meets all requirements (both in terms of hard requirements and "vibe")
  * We can ensure that the Organising Team has a good understanding of the local area and what is available in terms of transport, accommodation, areas to avoid, etc.
  * We can better connect with the local cloud native community in that area.

* **Cons/Risks:**
  * We may need to decide to not run a Cloud Native Rejekts event in a location where we don't have volunteers from the local area.
