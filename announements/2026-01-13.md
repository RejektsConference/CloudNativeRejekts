# The future of Cloud Native Rejekts

We’ve been hearing you loud and clear - you want Rejekts to continue and remain the amazing, community experience it’s always been. We’ve been blown away by the enthusiasm from the community following the news towards the end of last year, and we absolutely agree with you all! We’ve taken some time over the holidays to seriously think about how we can ensure Rejekts has a long and vibrant future, and we’re incredibly excited to share our plans.

## Introducing the Steering Committee!

To make this happen, we’ve put together what we’re calling for now the **Cloud Native Rejekts Steering Committee**, composed of: [Lexi Nadolski](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aleksateresanadolski/), [Marcus Noble](https://www.linkedin.com/in/marcusnoble/), and [Laura Santamaria](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lauraasantamaria/). This group’s sole purpose is to ensure the spirit of Rejekts continues, keep the focus firmly on what *you* want (a genuine community event) and ensure we continue to build something awesome, together! This group will be mostly hands-off and focus more on longevity of Rejekts and empowering the Organising Team to excel and succeed.

But what’s the Organising Team I hear you ask? Well…

## Local Teams Taking the Lead

Here’s the exciting part: We’re moving towards a model where each Rejekts conference will be run by a dedicated **Organising Team**! These teams will be built entirely of volunteers; ideally, we’d love to have people from the local area involved, and they’ll be responsible for making the conference happen - from securing the venue and sponsors to putting together a CFP (Call For Papers) program committee and running the show on the day.

Think of the Steering Committee as the guides, offering support and direction. The Organising Teams are the hands-on heroes, making the magic happen!

## About Amsterdam

We’re currently focused on making Rejekts for Amsterdam happen, and we’re determined to deliver an event there. However, we're realistic. With the timeframe, it’s a significant challenge. We’ll be working closely with the organising team to track progress, and, if it becomes clear that it’s not achievable, we’ll be upfront and honest with you. We’ll set a firm deadline with the organising team to assess the situation and make a final decision.

If (when? 😉) we *do* pull it off in time, expect a more lowkey experience compared to past events, focused on getting the community together and sharing knowledge. That’s always been the heart of Rejekts, and that's what we’re prioritising.

There is also still time to submit your CFP for a chance to speak - [https://sessionize.com/cloud-native-rejekts-eu-2026](https://sessionize.com/cloud-native-rejekts-eu-2026?ref=cloudnative.now) (Deadline 17th December)

## We Need Your Input!

This is a big shift, and we’re still working through the details, but it’s also a callback to how Rejekts started out. We really want to hear your thoughts and feedback! Let us know what you think, what you’d like to see, and how you’d like to get involved. For now, we have a Slack Channel ([\#Cloud-Native-Rejekts](https://cloud-native.slack.com/archives/C0A2DGPU8RH)) where we already have some enthusiastic folks gathered if you’d like to join the conversation and let us know your thoughts. You can also reach out to Lexi, Marcus or Laura through whatever channel suits you best to chat about your thoughts or to this announcement on socials.

If you want to volunteer to organize the Amsterdam Rejekts conference, we have a Google Form available to collect details: [https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd9j7R5-H\_AvVujOcZ-qFxFgqENFdnPkQHz5dBSSvLuhAtrhA/viewform](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd9j7R5-H_AvVujOcZ-qFxFgqENFdnPkQHz5dBSSvLuhAtrhA/viewform). We'll have more info for other Rejekts soon!

## The main goal? Rejekts continues to thrive!

We’re committed to building a sustainable model that ensures this event continues to be a fantastic space for learning, connection, and pushing the boundaries of cloud native.

We have ideas for how this could scale in the future and become bigger and better, but we’re staying realistic and taking things one step at a time.

We will have more updates when available and over the next few months you will see more things starting to come together. We plan to do this in the open as much as possible and will be moving everything to a public GitHub organisation where things like the website, sponsorship prospectus, event runbooks, volunteer signup, etc can all be maintained in the open.

Thank you for being part of the Rejekts community ♥️ let’s build this together!
