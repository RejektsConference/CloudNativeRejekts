# Sharing of attendee list with sponsors

**Status:** Approved

We WILL NOT share a list of attendee details (including, but not limited to, names, email addresses, and other personal information) with any sponsor.

We MAY allow sponsors on-site to scan attendees badges, or collect details another way, but only through the attendees explicit consent.

We MAY choose to share a list of companies that attendees are from, but this will be done in a manner that does not identify individuals.
